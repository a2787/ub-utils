## pakku.js

The Bilibili CRC32 UID candidate lookup is adapted from `crc32_crack.ts` in
[xmcp/pakku.js](https://github.com/xmcp/pakku.js), commit
`2cb6f52aba70d6b685aaff9a1c03aabec7f299b2`. The upstream project is licensed
under GPLv3 and credits the UID lookup work to `@dramforever`. OmniBlock's
adaptation was modified on 2026-08-21 for lazy initialization, typed bucket
indexes, forward CRC32 verification, explicit candidate labeling and manual
identity confirmation.
